Neon Break — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This policy describes how the Neon Break mobile game ("the app") handles information. Neon Break is developed by Bilal Ghanem ("the developer"). The short version: the app collects a small amount of anonymous gameplay analytics, and nothing that identifies you.

What the app collects

  • Anonymous gameplay analytics. When you play, the app sends a record of your session to the developer's own database (Supabase). A session record includes: when the session started and ended, its duration, the app version, your device's screen size and browser/WebView user-agent string, your device language, a random install ID generated on first launch (a random string stored on your device — it is not derived from any real identifier and is not linked to you), and gameplay outcomes such as score, turns played, bricks destroyed, and power-ups used.
  • Local storage. Your best score, unlocked achievements, seen tutorials, and settings (sound, music, haptics, screen shake) are saved on your device only. They are never sent anywhere and are removed if you uninstall the app or clear app data.

What the app does NOT do

  • It does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or any personal identifier.
  • It contains no ads and no third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • It does not sell or share data with any third party for advertising or any other purpose. Supabase acts only as a data processor hosting the developer's own analytics database.
  • It requires no account and asks for no permissions.

How data is used

Anonymous analytics are used solely to understand how the game is played (e.g., how far players get, which features are used, crash-free session rates) so the game can be improved. Data is transmitted over HTTPS (encrypted in transit) and stored in the developer's Supabase project with row-level security that prevents public access.

Data retention and deletion

Analytics rows are retained while they are useful for improving the game. Because the only key is a random install ID, the developer cannot tie any row to you. If you would like a session's records deleted, email the support address below with the approximate time you played and the rows will be removed. Uninstalling the app stops all further collection (and deletes the install ID from your device).

Children

The app contains no mature content and is expected to be rated Everyone. It does not knowingly collect any data from children beyond the anonymous analytics described above, which contain no personal information.
