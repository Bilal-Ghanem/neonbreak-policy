Neon Break — Request Data Deletion

Neon Break collects only anonymous gameplay analytics: session times, gameplay outcomes (score, turns), app version, device screen size and language, and a random install ID generated on first launch. It never collects your name, email, contacts, or anything that identifies you.

To request deletion of your gameplay data:

Send an email to bilalghanemengineer@gmail.com with the subject Neon Break — data deletion.

To help us locate your records, include the approximate dates/times you played (or nothing at all — we can delete by time range). We will delete every analytics row associated with your sessions and confirm by reply, typically within 72 hours.

Uninstalling the app also deletes the random install ID from your device and stops all further collection. See the privacy policy for full details.
